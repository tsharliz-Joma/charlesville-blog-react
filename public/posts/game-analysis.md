      How would I describe a Game Analysis ? 


      I would say a Game Analysis is a look in the many aspects of a game, in a way that looks to understand the mechanics that make a game function, from the the way it invites/how it invites players to keep playing or wonder about life and unmentioned rules that are simply accepted by the players to the cultural impact on society.
  - imageAlign: none
    heading: Why is Analysing games an important skill to have.
    text: From what I have read and to my understanding, the ability to analyse
      games enables you look at any or all game, take the lessons that are being
      taught either from a designers (how the games mechanics work) perspective
      or a players (the message I am being sent) and use the knowledge gained to
      the benefit you in designing games or living life.
